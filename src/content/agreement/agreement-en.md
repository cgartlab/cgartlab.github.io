## 7. CGArtLab FUI Data

CGArtLab FUI Data (the "plug-in") is an After Effects extension whose licence check requires a network connection. This section explains what it processes during activation, and the licence and refund rules.

### 7.1 Licence data

**Uploaded** (activation, renewal and spec requests only):

- **Machine-code hash**: a hash of an identifier derived from your hardware, used to distinguish devices and count licence seats. Only the hash is uploaded, **not the raw machine code**; we cannot reconstruct your device identifier from it.
- **Licence key**: to validate status and the number of bound devices.
- **Client version and request context**: plug-in version, IP address, user agent — used to diagnose activation failures and detect piracy or abuse.

**Never collected**: AE project files, composition structure, layer names or layer text; pre-renders or exports; clipboard contents, filesystem contents or screen contents.

**Network scope**: only licence checks need the network; rendering and export are fully offline, and an activated licence keeps working within its offline window when the network is unavailable.

This data is used solely for licence validation, device binding and support (such as moving a licence to a new machine), never for advertising profiles. We keep activation audit records to handle sharing, resale and refund disputes.

### 7.2 Licence and refunds

- **Two machines per key**: one key activates up to **2 computers** at the same time; contact support to deactivate one before moving to another.
- **7-day trial**: full functionality for 7 days after installation, then automatic fallback to the free categories (plain text, generic numbers, temperature, percentage). Projects that already used the plug-in data are unaffected.
- **Refunds**: handled under the rules of the channel you bought through. After a refund the key is deactivated, but **projects that already contain plug-in data still open and render normally**.
- **No sharing or resale**: keys are for the purchaser only; public sharing, resale or commercial redistribution may result in deactivation.

The complete terms are those shown at purchase and in the licence bundled with the plug-in; contact us with any questions.
