## Unexpected Turn of Events

Due to various coincidences, I joined a domestic film crew working on a theatrical movie production at the end of March this year. Because of this, the weekly has essentially turned into a quarterly publication. Recently, work pace has slowed down a bit, so I'm rushing to update this issue.

Throughout my career, this is the first time I've participated in such a large-scale project, and the pressure has been immense. Specific work content involves confidentiality and can't be discussed here. In short, this is the most complex and exhausting project I've ever worked on. Fortunately, the team consists of top experts from various fields—I learn something new almost every day, then encounter unprecedented problems. Being able to witness the industry's boundaries gradually advancing alongside such talented individuals is truly an honor.

Looking beyond the technical aspects, this kind of large-scale collaboration across different professions, industries, ages, and cognitive levels really tests the flexibility of personal workflows. I'll gradually share more after the confidentiality period ends.

## NotebookLM - Google's AI Note-Taking Application

![Google NotebookLM interface generating a podcast dialogue from uploaded source material](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596471386.webp)

While writing [[RSS and Modern Reading Habits (Part 2): Regaining Control of Information Collection]], I tried using it to research and organize materials about RSS and information overload. The results somewhat exceeded expectations—it could actually generate a 10-minute podcast based on provided materials (files, links, images you upload), featuring dialogue between male and female virtual characters, with surprisingly high completion quality, and in Chinese. Other highlights include generating note documents and mind maps based on specific topics.

## NoteGen - AI Note-Taking Application Focused on Recording and Writing

![NoteGen, an open-source lightweight AI note-taking application](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596488772.webp)

Another open-source AI note-taking application on GitHub (5k+ stars) focused on recording and writing direction. It's a free note tool emphasizing lightweight and clean experience, designed for users pursuing efficient and simple recording. Its installation package is only about ten megabytes, requiring no complex configuration for quick installation and use.

![NoteGen feature demonstration showing clean writing-focused interface](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596513798.webp)

The software is completely free with all features open, avoiding subscription fees or ad integration, while promising no bundled software, ensuring clean and interference-free user experience. From a product positioning perspective, Notegen focuses on "zero burden" as its core, providing basic note services suitable for users needing quick recording but not wanting to be disturbed by redundant features.

## SiliconFlow - Large Model Service Provider

![SiliconFlow AI infrastructure platform for accessing large language models via API](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596523544.webp)

I've been using their AI integration service since last year—the pricing is reasonable. Even after more than half a year, I still haven't used up the promotional credits they gave me; ten-something yuan can last two to three months.

**SiliconFlow** is a tech company focused on **AI infrastructure and toolchains**, dedicated to providing developers and enterprises with efficient solutions for AI development, training, and deployment. Its core direction centers on **large model optimization** and **AI engineering**.

For individuals, you can leverage its API to integrate into your own knowledge management tools and access the most advanced domestic models anytime.

What surprised me even more is that their pace of technical iteration far exceeded expectations. The **dynamic load balancing** feature launched just last month perfectly solved the response latency issues I'd been encountering when calling the API. By intelligently allocating compute resources, response speed now stays stable even during peak hours.

If you're interested, click [here](https://cloud.siliconflow.cn/i/09r0o1Ax) to register and claim a trial quota of 20 million tokens—let's grow together.

## Building a Brand – Redesigning a Business Start to Finish – YouTube How to Build a Brand

![Screenshot from Matthew Encina's Mode brand redesign YouTube video](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596537460.webp)

Matthew Encina joined Mode as Chief Design Officer in 2025, **leading a comprehensive brand redesign**. The goal is to reflect Mode's clearer vision and values, **enhance market distinctiveness and drive business growth**. He is a design industry predecessor I deeply admire; I've loved his content and have watched every single video since the sixth one he published on his channel.

![Mode brand redesign framework showing the Double Diamond design process](../_images/05-参与造梦的艺术：电影制作幕后与AI工具革新%20%20玄光周刊-1754596548192.webp)

The Mode brand redesign process in the video follows Matthew's self-created **Double Diamond framework**, progressing through exploration, definition, development, and delivery phases. Core concepts include **"warm technology"**, **"creating everyday joy"**, and **"creating products worth existing."** The brand visuals adopt a **deep green** primary palette, diverse typography, and warm photography style. The ultimate mission: **bring fun** to work and life, and **pioneer a "warm technology" niche market**. Currently, the **website has been redesigned**, and they're developing **eco-friendly packaging and next-generation products**. If you're building your own brand, I highly recommend studying their thinking and process—you'll definitely find inspiration.
